## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-10

- Convert between Cardano slots, POSIX time, dates and epochs on mainnet, preprod and preview.
- Era-aware conversion across Byron and Shelley+ parameters from official genesis presets.
- Helpers for epoch bounds and slot-in-epoch queries, with typed errors and `try*` variants.
- Zero runtime dependencies; ESM and CJS builds with TypeScript types.
